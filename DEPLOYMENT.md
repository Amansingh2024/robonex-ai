## 🌐 1-Click Deployment Options

### Option 1: InfinityFree (Free Hosting)
1. Log in to your [InfinityFree Control Panel](https://app.infinityfree.com/).
2. Open the **Online File Manager** for your account.
3. Open the `htdocs` folder.
4. Upload `robonex-ai-production.zip` and click **Extract**, or drag-and-drop all unzipped files directly into `htdocs`.
5. Visit your domain URL — your site is instantly live with HTTPS!

### Option 2: Netlify (Drag & Drop — 30 Seconds)
1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop the `robonex ai` folder (or unzip `robonex-ai-production.zip` and drop the folder).
3. Netlify will assign a live URL with free global CDN and SSL.

### Option 3: Vercel
```bash
npm i -g vercel
vercel --prod
```

### Option 4: cPanel / Apache / Hostinger
1. Go to **File Manager** -> `public_html`.
2. Upload `robonex-ai-production.zip`.
3. Extract inside `public_html`.
