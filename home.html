PARENT QUESTIONS & ADMISSION ADVICE

Everything parents ask about RoboNex AI.

Clear answers to common questions about prerequisites, hardware kits, weekend schedules, and trial sessions in Patna.

01 Kya mere bachhe ko pehle se coding ya computer aana chahiye?

Bilkul nahi! Hamare sabhi maker tracks zero-prerequisite hain. Ages 6–9 ke beginners visual drag-and-drop block coding (Scratch & PictoBlox) se start karte hain jisme animations aur basic games bante hain. Uske baad step-by-step real microcontrollers (Arduino, ESP32) par C++ aur Python AI computer vision sikhayi jati hai. Har student ko 1:6 ratio me personalized mentor attention milti hai.

02 Batches kab-kab hote hain aur timings kya hain?

Hamare paas do convenient options available hain:

  • Weekend Cohorts (Sat & Sun): Morning 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM & Evening 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  • Weekday Evening Slots (Tue to Fri): 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM

Schedules school homework aur exams ke sath conveniently balance karne ke liye design kiye gaye hain. Agar koi session miss hota hai to backup lab session provide kiya jata hai.

03 Kya hardware kit bachhe ghar le ja sakte hain?

Haan! Daily classes ke dauran students lab ke high-grade workstations, oscilloscope rigs, 3D printers aur robot kits par kaam karte hain. Iske sath Capstone Project ke dauran students ko personal take-home hardware kit provide ki jati hai jisse wo apna banaya hua robot, autonomous rover ya smart automation device ghar le jaakar family aur friends ko demonstrate kar sakein.

04 Free 45-Minute Trial Class me bachhe kya banayenge?

Complimentary Trial Class 100% hands-on hoti hai. Pehle hi din bachha breadboard par real DC motors, ultrasonic distance sensor aur LED circuitry wire karega, aur computer se command dekar working robot ko control karega. End me hamare senior mentor aapke bachhe ke logical aptitude aur interest ke hisaab se customized 12-week roadmap share karte hain.

05 Course completion par certificate aur competition opportunities milengi?

Haan! Har track complete hone par student ko Verified STEM & ISO Accredited RoboNex Certificate milta hai. Iske alawa hum students ko National Science Olympiad, World Robot Olympiad (WRO), aur Inter-School RoboWars ke liye tayyar karke mentor guidance provide karte hain.

06 Patna me lab kahan hai aur visit kaise kar sakte hain?

Hamara campus 26, 90 Feet Rd, Bhabha Colony, Kankarbagh, Hanuman Nagar, Patna, Bihar 800020 par sthit hai. Parents Monday se Sunday morning 9:30 AM se evening 7:30 PM ke beech direct visit kar sakte hain ya trial session reserve karne ke liye direct 9931023041 par call/WhatsApp kar sakte hain.
