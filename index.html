01 / COMPUTER VISION & NEURAL AI

Spatial AI & Real-Time Perception

Teaching computers to see and interpret the physical world. Students train OpenCV visual models to track optical contours, measure pixel velocities, segment objects by color, and command robotic pan-tilt turrets to track moving targets in under 15 milliseconds.

Engineering Realization: Students understand that artificial intelligence isn't magic—it's linear algebra, matrix transforms, and real-time camera sensor arrays.

02 / MECHATRONICS & AUTONOMY

Closed-Loop Kinematics & Motion

Giving digital code physical mechanical leverage. Students program dual-channel H-bridge drivers, calibrate optical wheel encoders, implement Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) closed loops, and balance inverted pendulum rovers over uneven obstacle tracks.

Engineering Realization: Students learn how sensors and math combine to keep mobile robots moving straight, negotiating sharp curves, and avoiding collisions.
▣Arduino Mega ↻High-Torque DC ⚡H-Bridge Driver ◈Ultrasonic Sonar
● LOW-LATENCY MQTT TELEMETRY
03 / DISTRIBUTED IOT & NETWORKS

Connected IoT Telemetry & Cloud Nodes

Connecting hardware to the global web. Students configure dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth mesh, transmit telemetry via lightweight MQTT brokers, and design responsive web dashboards to monitor remote soil sensors and smart relays.

Engineering Realization: Students build real industrial SCADA prototypes, understanding network packets, cloud APIs, and distributed wireless automation.
⌁MQTT Broker ☁Cloud Telemetry ▣ESP32 Wi-Fi ⌬Solid-State Relay
● 0.12MM ADDITIVE ACCURACY
04 / CAD ENGINEERING & 3D PRINTING

Parametric CAD & Additive Fabrication

From digital 3D model to physical machine part. Students learn parametric mechanical modeling in Fusion 360, calculate gear ratios, tolerances, and structural load factors, then manufacture physical chassis and custom gripper end-effectors on 3D printers.

Engineering Realization: Students discover that good software requires great mechanical design. A robot must be physically sound to perform in the real world.
3DFusion 360 CAD ✦3D Print Slicing ⚙Planetary Gears ⬡Tolerance Fits
